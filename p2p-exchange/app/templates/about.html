About P2P Exchange

What is this?

P2P Exchange is a no-KYC, non-custodial peer-to-peer platform for trading Monero (XMR). It allows users to buy and sell XMR directly with each other using any fiat payment method, without requiring identity verification, registration, or centralized custody of funds.

How does it work?

  1. Create or browse offers — Sellers post offers with their price, payment methods, and terms.
  2. Initiate a trade — A buyer finds an offer and starts a trade.
  3. Escrow — The seller sends XMR to an escrow address. The platform verifies the on-chain deposit.
  4. Fiat payment — The buyer sends fiat to the seller via the agreed payment method.
  5. Release — The seller confirms receipt and the escrowed XMR is released to the buyer.
  6. Dispute — If there's a problem, either party can open a dispute for arbitration.

Security

  • 🔒 No KYC — No registration, no email, no phone, no ID documents.
  • 🔑 Non-custodial — The platform never holds private keys.
  • 💬 E2E encrypted chat — All messages are encrypted client-side with NaCl box.
  • 🧅 Tor-friendly — No CDN, no external scripts, no tracking.
  • 🪙 Mnemonic backup — Optionally back up your identity with a 12-word BIP39 mnemonic.

Privacy

This platform is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect or store any personal information. Session tokens are HMAC-hashed before storage. Chat messages are encrypted end-to-end — the server stores only ciphertext.

Technology

  • Backend: Python (Flask)
  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript — no heavy frameworks
  • Encryption: X25519 + XChaCha20-Poly1305 (NaCl/libsodium)
  • Identity: BIP39 mnemonic → X25519 keypair
  • Network: Monero RPC (configurable supernode architecture)
