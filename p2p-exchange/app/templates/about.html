About P2P XMR Exchange

What is this?

P2P XMR Exchange is a no-KYC, non-custodial peer-to-peer exchange for trading Monero (XMR). It is fully self-hosted, works over Tor, and requires no email, phone number, or identity documents.

Your identity is a BIP39 mnemonic phrase. Your session is derived from your mnemonic via X25519 keypair derivation. All trade messages are end-to-end encrypted using NaCl (XChaCha20-Poly1305).

Security

  • No external CDN dependencies — all resources are self-hosted
  • Content Security Policy prevents XSS
  • All chat messages encrypted client-side before transmission
  • Session tokens are HMAC-SHA256 verified
  • Non-custodial: escrow is on-chain Monero
  • Tor hidden service compatible

FAQ

Is KYC required?

No. You only need a BIP39 mnemonic phrase. No email, phone, or identity documents.

Who holds the escrow?

The seller locks XMR in a Monero subaddress generated by the escrow service. Funds are released when the seller confirms receipt of fiat payment.

How is chat encrypted?

Messages are encrypted using X25519 Diffie-Hellman key exchange and XChaCha20-Poly1305 symmetric encryption (NaCl box). Only the two traders can decrypt messages.

What payment methods are accepted?

Any payment method agreed upon by the two traders: bank transfer, cash, mobile money, gift cards, etc.

Can I access this over Tor?

Yes. The platform is designed to work as a Tor hidden service. All resources are self-hosted; no external requests are made.

What if there's a dispute?

Either party can open a dispute. An administrator will review the trade evidence and resolve the dispute.
