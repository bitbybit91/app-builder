Magoradesk is a peer-to-peer (P2P) cryptocurrency trading platform that enables
users to buy and sell Bitcoin, Monero, Litecoin, and Ethereum directly with
other users.

Key Features:
• P2P cryptocurrency trading with multiple payment methods
• Support for Bitcoin (BTC), Monero (XMR), Litecoin (LTC), and Ethereum (ETH)
• Secure escrow system for safe trading
• Built-in wallet management
• Easy deposit and withdrawal functionality
• Real-time fee transparency (4% platform fee clearly displayed)

The platform applies a transparent 4% fee on all trades and deposits to
sustain platform operations and development. This fee is clearly displayed
before every transaction so users always know exactly what they are paying.

Magoradesk prioritizes user privacy and security, supporting privacy-focused
cryptocurrencies like Monero alongside other popular digital currencies.
